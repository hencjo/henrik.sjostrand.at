Community

  1. Brewing Agile 2012–2018

    I founded Brewing Agile in 2012. It grew out of the Scrum Beers crowd into a Göteborg conference about agile thinking, learning, leadership, collaboration, and process — less about selling a method, more about making space for practice and debate.

    2012 2013 2014 2015 2016 2017 2018

    Designed to be accessible and affordable, because Göteborg deserved quality content without people needing a corporate training budget or a trip abroad.

  2. Scrum Beers Göteborg Meetup

    The low-ceremony version of an agile meetup: people in a pub, talking about teams, delivery, collaboration, architecture, leadership, and whatever is currently making work harder than it needs to be.

    Deliberately relaxed: people comparing notes over a beer. Sometimes a theme; the side conversations often carry the evening.
