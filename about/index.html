About me

I’m Henrik, a developer living in Härryda, just outside Göteborg.

How it started

I’ve loved computers since 1994, and Linux has been my main operating system since 2003. Somewhere along the way the tinkering turned into a job, but it never really stopped being the tinkering.

I started doing open source back in the Java 6 days, and in 2012 I founded the Brewing Agile conference, which grew out of the Scrum Beers crowd here in Göteborg and ran until 2018.

What I do

I work as a developer, though these days most of my time goes into the bigger picture: the architecture and technical direction of connectivity platforms in telco that a lot of people rely on every day, from domain and API design all the way to running them in production. Much of it happens across team and company boundaries. I also wrote a standard interface between service providers and operators. I’m happy it has had an impact at all, and I believe it makes the business simpler for everyone involved.

The part I enjoy most is making systems simpler and sturdier, and pleasant to work on: builds that come out the same every time, environments that just start, tests you didn’t have to write by hand.

What I care about

Important inputs should be explicit, not discovered. Things should be reproducible, so that the same project gives the same result tomorrow. Dangerous operations should be boring: one direction, read-only by default, nothing destructive without asking. And a small tool that does one job well usually beats a large one that does everything almost.

Outside work

I have two kids, a house that is always a little bit of a DIY project, and a boat that is a DIY project too.
